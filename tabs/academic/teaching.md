## Summer Schools
### Instructor at Tapia Camps (Summer 2026)
A summer camp introducing high school students across Texas to various 
STEM topics. Alongside Prof. Paul Hand (the Tapia Camps director), I co-taught 
the "Techniques of a Pro Mathematician" program focused on developing some of the 
hard and soft skills needed to succeed in college math and beyond.
While teaching I learned a bunch, gained some invaluable experience in effective 
and engaging pedagogy, and grew a deeper appreciation for K-12 teachers. 

## Teaching Assistant Courses
### ELEC 330: Random Signals @ Rice (Fall 2023/24/25)
A first course in probability and random processes for ECE undergrads focusing on
discrete and continuous random variables, laws of large numbers, 
the central limit theorem, and basic estimation theory.

### 18-290: Signals and Systems @ CMU (Fall 2021 & Spring 2022)
A first course in signal processing focusing on discrete and continuous 
signal representations, linear time-invariant systems, convolutions, basic 
Fourier theory, and sampling.

### 18-491/691: Fundamentals of Signal Processing @ CMU (Spring 2021)
A second course in signal processing further exploring relationships between 
discrete and continuous signal representations and related transforms 
(Fourier Series, Fourier Transforms, z-transforms, short-time), filter design, 
time-frequency representations, multi-rate processing, and the Fast Fourier Transform.
