In life, and math, it is often fruitful to view things from different perspectives.
In probability theory, for instance, we can often learn a lot about an event by looking at its complement.
In this spirit, to give you a better sense of who I am[^care], here's a list of who I am not[^stephen-wang]:

    * [Carlos Tavares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Tavares)

    * [Carlos Taveras](https://www.baseballamerica.com/players/413469-carlos-taveras/)
    * [Carlos Taveras](https://www.milb.com/player/carlos-taveras-829056)

    * [Carlos D. Tavares](https://www.carlosdtavares.com/)[^duke]
